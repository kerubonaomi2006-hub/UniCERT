👤 General Users (2.3.1):
Students, lecturers, campus staff. Can report incidents, view submission confirmation, track tickets, and access basic containment instructions. No administrative access.

👑 Administrators (2.3.2):
Campus security governance. Access the administrative dashboard, review stored incident records, inspect risk assessments, and manage authorized user accounts.
