Platform Capabilities

What UniCERT Does For You

A unified response platform for every member of the university community.

🚨
Instant Incident Reporting
Submit phishing, malware, unauthorized access, data breaches and more through a guided 2-minute form with automatic category classification.
📊
Real-Time Risk Assessment
Multi-factor NIST CSF 2.0 engine scores every incident as CRITICAL / HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW with SLA timers and auto-generated containment playbooks.
🔍
Ticket Tracking
Track your reported incident end-to-end. View investigation status updates, assigned technician, and official safety instructions in real-time.
🛡️
SOC Triage Console
IT Personnel get a full Security Operations Centre view: IoCs, audit logs, triage queue, assignee management, and KENET-CERT dossier export.
👑
Executive Governance
Administrators oversee institution-wide posture, manage user access tiers, review statutory compliance (ODPC 72h), and audit all incident records.
🗄️
PostgreSQL Database
Every report, user account, triage note and assessment is stored directly in PostgreSQL — durable, queryable, and audit-trail ready.
